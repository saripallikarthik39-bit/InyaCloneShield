The Critical Threat Landscape

Why Indian Voice Banking is Vulnerable

1. AI Voice Clone Clones (Vocoders)

With just 3 seconds of leaked audio from social media or WhatsApp, generative AI models can clone a victim's exact tone. Scammers use these clones over telephony to request urgent transfers.

2. The "Two Humans" Challenge

A real human caller speaking with a stolen account number. Traditional Speech Recognition (ASR) only understands WHAT is spoken; it fails to verify WHO physically spoke it.

⚠️ Telemetry Reality: 8kHz G.711 compression strips high frequencies, making software-only defenses fail unless physical vocal tract biological formants are evaluated.
