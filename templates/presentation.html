Technical Scrutiny

Real-World Disadvantages & Limitations

Acknowledging honest acoustic and biological challenges in real-world telephony

1. Severe Illness / Cold & Cough

Acute laryngitis or nasal congestion changes vocal cord mass and nasal cavity resonance, shifting fundamental frequency ($F_0$) temporarily.

2. Extreme Acoustic Noise (<5dB SNR)

Calls made from roaring railway platforms or loud industrial machinery can contaminate higher formant bands (F2 & F3).

3. Text-Independent Vowel Shifts

Different spoken words naturally carry different vowel formants (/a/ vs /i/ vs /u/), causing variance in unconstrained dialogues.

4. Long-Term Voice Aging

Over 5 to 10 years, human larynx cartilage elasticity gradually shifts, slightly modifying baseline timbre over decades.

Engineering Solutions

How Inya-CloneShield Overcomes Each Limitation

Robust fail-safes ensuring zero legitimate customers are locked out

Fix for Illness & Hoarseness:

Dynamic Confidence Fallback

If voice match score is borderline (50%–70%), the system does NOT lock the account; it automatically triggers the Out-of-Band Voice-OTP phone call to verify the user friction-free.

Fix for Extreme Noise (<5dB):

Spectral Gating & Wiener Filtering

Applied pre-FFT noise subtraction on 8kHz PCM chunks to isolate stationary noise floors before computing formant filterbanks.

Fix for Text-Independent Variance:

Phoneme Alignment via Prisma ASR

Leveraging Gnani Prisma ASR phoneme alignment to extract and compare spectral formants on matched vowel syllables regardless of sentence spoken.

Fix for Long-Term Voice Aging:

Rolling Exponential Moving Average (EMA)

On every successful authentication (>95%), the customer's centroid vector is updated by 2%, keeping the voiceprint perpetually synced.
