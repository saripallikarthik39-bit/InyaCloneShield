01. Problem Statement & Threat Vector

Conversational AI banking bots are booming in India, but traditional IVRs authenticate only knowledge (Account #, OTP), not biological identity.

  • AI Synthetic Voice Clones: Neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, ElevenLabs) clone victim voices from just 3-second audio clips.
  • The "Two Humans" Blindspot: Impostor friends or relatives recite genuine customer details over phone calls, bypassing knowledge tests.
02. The Inya-CloneShield Solution

  • Layer 2 (7-Band Formant Timbre): Matches 7 distinct resonant spectral formant cavities against enrolled user baselines with >96% accuracy.
